<h1>Seungjun Lee</h1>
<p class="role">Research Scientist · Asan Medical Center</p>
<p>I am a Research Scientist in healthcare AI specializing in <b>deep generative models for anomaly detection</b>, <b>cross-modality medical image synthesis</b>, and <b>multimodal foundation models</b>. My work spans clinical problem definition, model development, and regulatory-grade deployment.</p>
<p>At Asan Medical Center, I work on clinical vision-language foundation models and generative anomaly detection for emergency brain CT triage. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at DeepMetrics (clinical time-series modeling for ICU mechanical ventilation) and VUNO Inc. (multimodal diffusion models for synthesizing amyloid and tau PET from MRI, and core engineering for FDA-cleared <span style="white-space:nowrap">VUNO Med®-DeepBrain™</span>), and a Machine Learning Engineer at MakinaRocks.</p>
