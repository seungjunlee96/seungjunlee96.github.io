<h1>Seungjun Lee</h1>
<p class="role">Research Scientist · VUNO Inc.</p>
<p>I build AI systems for medical imaging, with a focus on <b>anomaly detection</b>, <b>deep generative models</b>, and <b>multimodal foundation models</b>. At VUNO I lead neuroimaging diagnostics research, adapting foundation models to specific clinical modalities. Before that I built production MLOps pipelines at MakinaRocks and did my M.S. research at Asan Medical Center.</p>
<p>My work has appeared in <i>Nature Communications</i> and received the Pfizer Medical Research Award.</p>
